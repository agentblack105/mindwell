Understanding Mental Health

Depression
More than just sadness. Depression is a common but serious mood disorder that affects how you feel, think, and handle daily activities. Symptoms include persistent sadness, loss of interest in activities, changes in appetite, and difficulty concentrating.
Learn more →
Anxiety
When worry becomes overwhelming. Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear or worry that interferes with daily life. This includes generalized anxiety, panic attacks, and social anxiety. Physical symptoms may include racing heart, sweating, and trembling.
Learn more →
PTSD
Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder can develop after experiencing a traumatic event. Symptoms include flashbacks, nightmares, severe anxiety, and uncontrollable thoughts about the event. It's common in Nigeria due to various forms of trauma.
Learn more →
Bipolar Disorder
A condition characterized by extreme mood swings including emotional highs (mania) and lows (depression). In Nigeria, it's often misdiagnosed or attributed to spiritual causes. Early recognition is crucial for effective management.
Learn more →

When to Seek Help

If you've experienced any of these symptoms for more than two weeks, consider speaking with a mental health professional:

  • • Persistent sadness
  • • Loss of interest
  • • Sleep changes
  • • Appetite changes
  • • Difficulty concentrating
  • • Thoughts of self-harm
